What is Ashok Home Furnishing?

Ashok Home Furnishing is the only authorized D'Decor dealer in Vellore, Tamil Nadu. It supplies and fits custom curtains, blinds, wallpapers, sofas, mattresses, headboards, carpets and wooden flooring for homes in Vellore, Katpadi and nearby areas.

Customers visit the Perumugai experience centre or the Fort branch on Old Bypass Road to see fabrics and designs in person. A free site visit is available if you would rather have the team come to your home and show options against your own walls, windows and furniture.

The business has furnished 100+ homes in and around Vellore and is rated 4.8 on Google and 5.0 on Justdial.

FAQ

Questions we hear most

Is Ashok Home Furnishing an authorized D'Decor dealer in Vellore?
Yes. Ashok Home Furnishing is the only authorized D'Decor dealer in Vellore, with showrooms in Perumugai and on Old Bypass Road near Fort.
Where are the showrooms in Vellore?
The Perumugai experience centre is at 85/10 A, Anna Nagar, opposite Balaji Honda on NH-48, Vellore 632009. The Fort branch is at No. 6, Old Bypass Road, opposite Surana Hardware, Thottapalayam, Vellore 632004.
What are the showroom timings?
The showroom is listed as open from 10 am to 8:30 pm. Please call or WhatsApp before visiting to confirm timings on holidays.
What products do you sell?
Curtains and drapes, blinds, sofas and couches, lounge chairs, centre tables, poufs and ottomans, headboards, designer cots, latex mattresses, bedding, wallpapers, carpets, carpet tiles, rugs and wooden flooring.
